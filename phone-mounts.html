Which mount type suits your car?

Vent mounts are compact and hide well but can block airflow. Dash and windshield mounts are the most stable and sit highest in your sightline. Magnetic mounts are the fastest to use — snap and go — but need a metal plate or a MagSafe-compatible case. Cup-holder arms rescue cars with cluttered dashes.

If you drive an iPhone 12 or newer, a MagSafe mount gives you perfect alignment and wireless charging in one motion, which is hard to beat for daily convenience.

A note on charging

Wireless car charging is convenient but slower and warmer than a cable — fine for topping up on a commute, less ideal if you're running GPS and music on a long haul. For heavy navigation use, a mount plus a good USB-C cable keeps the battery ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Which mount type is best?

Vent mounts are compact, dash mounts are the most stable, and magnetic mounts are the fastest to use. Pick based on your dash layout and how you like to dock.

Do magnetic mounts hurt the phone?

No. The magnets don't affect modern phones or their storage, but they do need a magnet plate or a MagSafe-compatible case to grip.

Will a MagSafe mount charge through a case?

Only through thin, MagSafe-certified cases. Thick or non-magnetic cases block proper alignment and wireless charging.
